# Design Context

## Audience

Academic peers, potential collaborators, research institutes, funders, journalists, and members of the public interested in AI governance, public administration, comparative politics, and digital society.

## Primary use cases

1. Understand Ming Ma's identity, institutional affiliation, and research agenda in under a minute.
2. Explore current research projects and methods.
3. Scan recent updates and peer-reviewed publications.
4. Open papers, download the CV, or initiate contact.

## Brand personality

Rigorous, credible, internationally minded, and intellectually adventurous. The presentation combines an academic editorial voice with a restrained retro-futurist particle world inspired by Dala. It should feel like a serious researcher's site, not an AI startup landing page.

## Visual signature

A side-profile humanoid researcher wearing a broad mixed-reality visor, rendered as interactive hollow triangular particles. Silver hair is tied high into a long ponytail that falls vertically. The ear is anatomically human. Night Archive palette: midnight ink, warm ivory, ultraviolet, deep teal, archival amber.
